# Vaccine

`Alchemical` `Consumable` `Elixir` `Healing`

**Bulk** -

**Usage** held in 1 hand

**Activate** 1 action (manipulate)

A vaccine grants a creature an item bonus or immunity on all saving throws vs a
specific disease for a full year, and related diseases may get bonuses as well,
depending on their level. For instance, a moderate vaccine (6) against a
Harpy's Putrid Plague (5) would give a +2 bonus against that specific disease,
and immunity against a Giant Rat's Putrid Plague (-1).

The bonus or immunity depends on the level of the disease and vaccine as
shown in the table below.

| Vaccine level             | Specific Disease | Related Disease |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Same as disease           | +1               | -               |
| 1 over disease            | +2               | -               |
| 2 over disease            | +4               | +1              |
| 3 over disease            | Immunity         | +2              |
| 4 over disease            | Immunity         | +3              |
| 5 over disease            | Immunity         | +4              |
| 6 and higher over disease | Immunity         | Immunity        |

**Special** A vaccine is the same rarity as the disease it's designed to
prevent, or as the creature who inflicts the disease if the disease itself
doesn't list a rarity. For an `Uncommon` vaccine, the price is doubled. For a
`Rare` vaccine the price is x5. For a `Unique` vaccine the price is x10.

**Craft Requirements** Creating a vaccine requires a sample of the disease in
question.
